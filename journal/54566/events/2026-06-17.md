- The issue is that test 201 uses @Transactional (changed from @WithTransaction on main). With @Transactional, the transaction is external (opened by TransactionalContextPool), and HR wraps it in ExternalTransaction. ExternalTransaction.markForRollback() is purely informational per HR design: "the external transaction owner is responsible for the actual commit/rollback lifecycle." The Quarkus TransactionalInterceptorBase.commit() was committing the raw Vert.x transaction without checking isMarkedForRollback().
- Added hibernate-reactive worktree to 54566 on the 3.4 branch. Both fail, confirming that markForRollback on ExternalTransaction does not prevent data from being committed. This is by design in HR, not a bug.
